# Retired dictionary review workflows

The local review interface and its V1, WordNet and Mahan Kosh queues are
historical authoring work. October 8 Dictionary v2 frequency/sense policies
are also retired as release inputs. Their approvals, exclusions and pending
items do not modify the October 9 owner-approved snapshot.

Use [approved dictionary sources and maintenance](definition_sources.md) for
current imports and integrity checks. All words and selected definitions in
the current source release were approved by the owner. Do not reopen old
queues, run their bulk decision commands or require another content approval
as part of an ordinary rebuild.

Historical reports and milestones retain their original scope. Future content
changes require a newly approved source release. No local editorial server,
raw dictionary or review queue belongs in runtime assets.
